mansoura-map.json is a derived OpenStreetMap database with a bounded extract,
local meter projection, graph connectivity, and explicitly marked inferred road
widths/building heights. Geographic road coordinates and mapped building
footprints are retained. Procedural facade decoration is not a surveyed facade.
Source query, endpoint, retrieval timestamp, projection and data limitations are
included in the JSON provenance. The original downloaded source is retained at
data/source/mansoura-osm-source.json and can be regenerated with
python scripts/acquire_mansoura.py (dependency: scripts/requirements.txt).
