# Mansoura Taxi: reference notes

Research checked on 15 October 2026. This prototype uses an original, compressed city layout and procedural models informed by the references below. It is a stylized interpretation of old Mansoura, not a surveyed digital twin. Distances, road geometry, building footprints, traffic and landmark placements are approximations for play. No third-party game code, textures, recordings or photographs were bundled as game assets.

## What makes this Mansoura

- **Damietta Nile branch, with Talkha opposite.** Use a broad river and a dense opposite-bank skyline, not a sea horizon or beach. Mansoura is on the east bank; the river curves around the city's northern/western side. The Nile photos below are useful for the mix of closely packed brick/concrete midrises, palms, riverfront traffic and sunset color.
- **Al-Mashaya and Al-Gomhouria.** The lower riverfront street, shops and promenade form the city's recognizable driving corridor. Local reporting describes parallel lower Al-Mashaya and Al-Gomhouria routes. Do not confuse these with the Mediterranean waterfront in **New Mansoura**, a separate coastal city.
- **Mansoura taxi livery.** A burgundy/maroon body with cream or white sections is a strong local cue. The model in this game is original; the reference photograph is listed below. The yellow-and-black taxi from Alexandria Taxi is specific to that game's setting.
- **University district.** Mansoura University's own contact page places it on Al-Gomhouria Street. Its official gallery describes a green campus, and the university offers a 360-degree film and a map. Campus gates, greenery, students and hospital traffic provide a useful change from dense downtown blocks.
- **Shenawy Palace.** Use an original simplified Italianate landmark with a red-brick core, ivory columns and balustrades, broad entry stairs and garden trees. Reference photographs depict two principal floors above a basement. Architectural detail and exact placement are approximated.
- **Dar Ibn Luqman.** A historic courtyard house/museum downtown is a different scale from the palace. Commons lists it on Port Said Street, with coordinates near 31.045217, 31.379953. A compact shaded heritage courtyard/arched entry is a suitable stylized cue.
- **Bridges.** Road and railway crossings to Talkha, plus a university-area bridge, are visible in the river photo collections. Keep bridge names and types distinct when expanding the map. The current game compresses these features.

Arabic signs to use naturally: **المنصورة** (Mansoura), **المشاية** (Al-Mashaya), **شارع الجمهورية** (Al-Gomhouria), **جامعة المنصورة** (Mansoura University), **كوبري طلخا** (Talkha Bridge), **قصر الشناوي** (Shenawy Palace), **دار ابن لقمان** (Dar Ibn Luqman), **مسجد الموافي** (Al-Mowafi Mosque), **نادي جزيرة الورد** (Geziret El-Ward Club). Generic original shop names avoid implying endorsement by a real business.

## Reference game mechanics

- [Dayeri's official about page](https://dayeri.com/about) describes Cairo ring-road driving, Egyptian mixed traffic, missions, casual driving, timed play, vehicle choices and upgrades. [Its controls page](https://dayeri.com/howto) describes steering/control choices and vehicle condition. Useful inspiration: instant browser play, readable road signs, dense varied traffic, locally grounded language and satisfying short driving sessions.
- [Alexandria Taxi's official about page](https://alextaxigame.com/about/) describes taxi pickups and fares along the Corniche between Qaitbay and Montaza, recognizable landmarks, fuel stops, vehicle upgrades, traffic, pedestrians, radio and additional race/online modes. Useful inspiration: a distinctive Egyptian waterfront, destinations with local identity and a clear taxi-job loop.
- Mansoura Taxi's assets, city layout and implementation are original. GTA-style inspiration concerns a cinematic chase camera, polished driving interface and a freely explorable city atmosphere; no Rockstar assets, branding or map content are included.

## Open map data path

[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=14/31.047/31.383) is a practical future source for street centerlines, rivers and building footprints. The [official copyright page](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) states that data is available under ODbL and explains attribution and share-alike requirements. Display “© OpenStreetMap contributors” with the linked copyright page if OSM data is incorporated, and preserve the derived data/source method as required by that license. This prototype does not import OSM geometry or use OSM tiles.

A future import should use a small bounded data extract and local cached geometry, with manual verification of incomplete names/heights. Avoid scraping Google map imagery or treating public tile servers as a game's unlimited asset backend. Survey-grade replication would also need up-to-date licensed imagery or original field capture; street footage alone does not supply exact dimensions or complete unseen geometry.
